# Foushee Justice Action-Interpretation Candidates V3

Status: implemented locally; candidate review decision pending.

## Milestone intent

Regenerate the 37-action M3A candidate set after the externally governed
`global_revision_required` decision. The revision retains the V2 field model
while replacing candidate-confirming coverage review with an independently
frozen, source-first expected inventory; adding exact-version differential and
cross-field consistency reviews; and correcting the reviewed S. 5 and H.R. 1156
failures.

## Scope and authority

The work is bound to repository baseline
`24a2bcb37347f74c6c40261930024e85676cd8d0` and the frozen M2 source-readiness
artifact. It preserves the V1 and V2 roots byte-for-byte. V3 is candidate-only,
non-authorizing, non-public, and unavailable to runtime selectors.

This milestone does not authorize M3B, acceptance, canonical interpretation
state, episodes, propositions, Semantic IR, synthesis, persistence,
publication, deployment, remote data access, or evidence acquisition.

## Execution sequence

1. Verify the baseline, V1/V2 snapshot ancestry and bytes, offline sentinel,
   M1/M2 authority, and predecessor validators.
2. Generate fresh M2-bound worker packets and 37 benchmark-blind initial
   candidates without reading V1/V2 candidate outputs.
3. Derive and freeze candidate-inaccessible expected provision and limit
   inventories with distinct expected-item identities and governed locators.
4. Compare candidates to those inventories; independently review related-action
   differentials, cross-field consistency, scope, and neutrality.
5. Apply one evidence-bound global correction cycle, preserving initial and
   final candidates plus the exact two field-level correction records.
6. Freeze the complete final batch and its content-subject digest.
7. Only after freeze, compare seven benchmarks, derive deterministic random,
   challenge, and contrast samples, generate the dossier, and generate parity
   last.
8. Validate schemas, blindness, regressions, source binding, routing, freeze,
   sampling, final bytes, and the absence of runtime/public selection.

- All 37 governed actions have fresh V3 packets, inventories, candidates, and
  independent reviews.
- S. 5 contains all six governed detention-trigger categories and remains
  differentiated from H.R. 29.
- H.R. 1156 consistently represents four named pandemic unemployment programs.
- Roll 155 remains ambiguous with the source-identity conflict visible; roll
  278 remains `no_safe_candidate`.
- No proposed candidate retains a major or critical review finding.
- The frozen batch precedes benchmark access and is not mutated post-freeze.
- Every final JSON/dossier path has independently recomputed content-subject and
  serialized-file hashes in a last-generated passing parity manifest.
- An empty, non-authorizing human decision template requests exactly one of
  `generalization_pass`, `global_revision_required`, or
  `generalization_rejected`.
- One local snapshot commit is created; no push, PR, merge, database, network,
  persistence, publication, or deployment occurs.
