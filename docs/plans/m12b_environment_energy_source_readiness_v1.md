# Milestone Plan: M12B Environment & Energy Source Readiness V1

## Intent And Outcome

- Evaluate interpretation-source readiness for exactly the 63 actions authorized
  by the detached M12A Environment & Energy universe receipt.
- Produce content-addressed official evidence and closed neutral projections for
  a later, separately authorized interpretation milestone.
- Stop with one draft PR for independent review; authorize no semantic,
  publication, runtime, database, or production effect.

## Scope And Decision Envelope

- In scope: authority-bound acquisition, generic readiness evaluation, schema
  cardinality generalization, one readiness record per approved action,
  independent validation and adversarial tests, current-state reconciliation,
  documentation, commit, push, and a draft PR.
- Out of scope: reopening M12A membership; acquiring for the 25 unresolved
  actions; action meaning; Support/Opposition; episodes; Semantic IR; synthesis;
  public wording; frontend/runtime behavior; persistence; publication;
  deployment; and production/database access.
- Established source classes, role semantics, six readiness states, and blocker
  precedence govern. Unsupported or stage-incompatible operative evidence must
  fail closed without a new Environment-specific semantic rule.
- The protected user-owned ZIPs are excluded from every read, inspection,
  hashing, staging, packaging, and mutation operation.

## Definition Of Done

- [ ] The receipt's exact 63 approved IDs appear once each and only once; all 25
  unresolved IDs and every outside action are absent.
- [ ] Receipt, proposal, selection, source inventory, cutoff, complete official
  action set, membership, and universe-subject identities remain content-bound.
- [ ] Every action has exact member-action, action-identity/stage, and operative-
  content role accounting plus one of the six closed readiness states.
- [ ] Raw official evidence is content-addressed separately from neutral,
  digest-bound projections; forbidden semantic or partisan fields are absent.
- [ ] The schema is cardinality-generic and the unchanged 82-action M11B
  National Security artifact still validates.
- [ ] Current state preserves completed M11B history, records M12B as
  source-readiness-only, and keeps all later authority gates false.
- [ ] Dedicated adversarial tests and the requested broader regression matrix
  pass, with behavior and final diff inspected.
- [ ] One scoped commit is pushed and one draft PR is open at the exact tested
  head; work stops before action interpretation.

## Discoveries And Decisions

- The generic evaluator already implements the required six states and
  deterministic blocker precedence; no new readiness ontology is needed.
- The M11B builder is milestone-specific acquisition/composition code over that
  generic evaluator. M12B may reuse its source patterns while binding only the
  M12A paths and action IDs.
- Cardinality constants belong in the milestone validator, not the reusable JSON
  schema. All source, stage, neutrality, digest, and enum constraints remain
  unchanged.
- Validation of an already governed artifact reads each committed Clerk XML only
  after its raw digest matches both the approved proposal and source inventory.
  The `.local` Clerk cache remains a builder input, not a hosted-validation
  dependency.
