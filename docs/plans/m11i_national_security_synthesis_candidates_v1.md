Status: implementation and local validation complete; draft PR preparation.

## Intent

Build a detached, deterministic, non-authorizing synthesis-candidate package
from the exact human-accepted M11H canonical internal Behavioral Semantic IR.
Stop for human substantive synthesis review before acceptance or public wording.

## Outcome

- A generic synthesis-candidate compiler and fail-closed validator.
- A concise governed review package with complete 15-proposition accounting.
- Exact M11H proposition, implementation-record, episode, and action lineage
  without evidence inflation.

## Scope And Boundaries

- In scope: generic candidate contract, National Security candidate definitions,
  deterministic artifacts, schemas, dossier, tests, current-state record, and a
  draft PR.
- Out of scope: synthesis acceptance, public wording, publication, persistence,
  database or production writes, deployment, and runtime changes.
- Accepted M11A-M11H and Justice artifacts remain unchanged.
- The protected Justice ZIP remains untracked and untouched.

## Decision Envelope

- Candidate synthesis may relate only exact accepted M11H propositions.
- Excluded notable choices may contribute only explicit contextual, contrast,
  or limiting roles; limiting inputs cannot be promoted.
- Topic similarity, motive, ideology, party, and raw vote direction are not
  synthesis authority.
- Any synthesis acceptance or downstream authority requires later human review.
