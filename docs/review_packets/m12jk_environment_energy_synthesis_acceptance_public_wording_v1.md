## Proposed public wording

### Environment & Energy

Across 13 resolutions, Foushee repeatedly opposed congressional efforts to
overturn EPA California vehicle-emissions waiver decisions, Energy Department
appliance and equipment rules, and Bureau of Land Management land decisions.

The underlying decisions differed, and these 13 resolutions are one part of the
broader reviewed Environment & Energy record.

### Congressional efforts to overturn agency decisions

Across three repeated patterns covering 13 resolutions, Foushee opposed
congressional efforts to overturn separate EPA California vehicle-emissions
waiver decisions, Energy Department appliance and equipment rules, and Bureau
of Land Management land decisions.

The underlying decisions covered different rules and land actions, and these
votes do not show support for every part of them or for the agencies generally.

### California vehicle-emissions waivers

Across two resolutions, Foushee opposed congressional efforts to overturn two
separate EPA California vehicle-emissions waiver decisions.

The resolutions involved different waiver decisions and emissions standards;
the votes do not show support for every part of the underlying rules.

### Appliance and commercial-equipment rules

Across four resolutions, Foushee opposed congressional efforts to overturn
separate Energy Department rules for appliances and commercial equipment,
including standards, certification, labeling, and enforcement.

The rules covered different products and functions; these votes do not show
support for every requirement or for regulation generally.

### Bureau of Land Management decisions

Across seven resolutions, Foushee opposed congressional efforts to overturn
separate Bureau of Land Management decisions involving land plans, leasing,
activity plans, or withdrawals.

The decisions covered different places and actions; these votes do not show
support for every part of each decision or for BLM policy generally.
