## 6329b predicates and scope

**(b)(2)(A)** requires an agency determination that continued presence during an
investigation/notice period **may**: **(i)** threaten the employee or others;
**(ii)** destroy investigation-relevant evidence; **(iii)** cause loss/damage to
Government property; **or(iv)** otherwise jeopardize legitimate Government
interests. The alternatives are disjunctive. The complete(b)(2)also requires
considering specified alternatives under(B), and determining none is appropriate
under(C). Those general agency requirements are preserved as context, not
automatically imported into the narrower403caller.

The complete definition matters: **(a)(5)(B)(ii) excludes the Inspector General
of an agency from this section's employee definition**. This is distinct from
**(c)(4)** procedures for employees **of** an OIG. Neither that exclusion nor the
general leave machinery is used here to override/resolve403's express caller.

Other operative context remains available: initial investigative leave up to
30**work days** after the referenced10workday period; notice leave limited to
the notice period; written notice and next-day action; initial additional leave
extensions up to30workdays with90workday **additional** cap/approval conditions;
further30workday periods require certification and a report within5**business
days**, and cannot be further extended on/after30**calendar days** after completed
