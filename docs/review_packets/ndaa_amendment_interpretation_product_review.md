## A. Pattern Summary

These 17 amendment votes collectively cover a single House NDAA amendment cluster. They are not a full National Security & Foreign Policy record.

Substance covered:

- War powers: repeal of the 2002 and 1991 AUMFs.
- Military health care and military-installation sex/gender policy: several amendments on TRICARE, the Exceptional Family Medical Program, service-academy athletics, forms/surveys, and single-sex spaces.
- Defense administration and installation rules: base flag authority, installation security penalties, and DOD reporting.
- Procurement / contracting / administration: vehicle-propulsion preferences and recruitment-contract restrictions.
- Foreign policy / security assistance: Ukraine assistance, Taiwan security funding, and overseas humanitarian/disaster/civic aid.
- Environment / defense operations: offshore wind radar certification and Endangered Species Act / critical-habitat rules affecting military or National Guard lands.
- Other NDAA-linked amendment topics: DOD cell-cultured meat research/procurement/promotion.
