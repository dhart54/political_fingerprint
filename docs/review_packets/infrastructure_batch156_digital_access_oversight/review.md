**house:119:1:107: H.R.1402: ticket-price, resale and digital-platform rules**

The whole House text would require sellers and resale exchanges to display total ticket price when a price is displayed in advertising, disclose it from the first ticket display throughout purchase, and itemize base price and each required fee before purchase completion. Required ticket fees include taxes and listed processing/delivery/facility charges; optional products/services unnecessary to use or possess the ticket are excluded. It prohibits selling/offering/advertising a ticket without actual OR constructive possession, but permits a separate ticket-obtaining service by secondary issuers/exchanges if clearly identified as a service, distinctly separated throughout purchase and disclosed before selection as not guaranteeing a ticket. Resale status must be disclosed before purchase. Affiliation/endorsement claims require an executed partnership agreement OR express written consent; venue names, including misspellings, in secondary sellers/exchanges domain names or subdomains require venue-owner authorization. Cancellation/postponement provisions have an express beyond-reasonable-control exception. They require full total-price refunds for covered cancellation; subject to availability, same/comparable replacement tickets for postponements of no more than six months when originals are invalid; and for postponements over six months a purchaser option between full refund and a qualifying replacement when the original is invalid. Guarantee/refund policy, fee-refund circumstances and how to obtain refunds must be disclosed. Sections2/3/5 expressly use180 days after enactment; no180-day timing is supplied to section4, which lacks that wording. FTC must report within six months on BOTS Act enforcement, coordination/challenges and recommendations. Violations receive section18(a)(1)(B) rule-violation treatment; FTC enforcement, statutory privileges/immunities and other authority are preserved literally without calculating a penalty. The event definition requires a live activity at a venue EXCEEDING200 capacity, public access AND the specified interstate-commerce relationship. Incorporated BOTS definitions include physical/electronic/other ticket evidence, future-event purchase entitlements and several ticket-issuer types; the dated BOTS statute has anti-circumvention/resale predicates and enforcement/security-research exceptions, not a universal software ban. Full FTC Act enforcement and current-law integration are unclaimed. The bill does not show actual seller misconduct, ban all resale or guarantee refunds for every cancellation.
