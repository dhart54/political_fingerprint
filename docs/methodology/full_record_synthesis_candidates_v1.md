# Full-Record Synthesis Candidates V1

This contract compiles detached synthesis candidates only from human-accepted,
canonical internal Behavioral Semantic IR propositions.

Each candidate uses typed `semantic_role = synthesis` relationships. Accepted
behavioral proposition content is referenced by implementation-record and
content digests and is never rewritten. Underlying accepted episodes and actions
are derived as traceability lineage; proposition nodes and their episodes are
not counted as separate independent evidence.

Excluded notable choices may appear only as contextual, contrast, or limiting
inputs. Limiting propositions cannot be promoted. Raw actions, raw votes,
contrast-only episodes, and no-safe episodes cannot enter synthesis directly.

The package accounts for every accepted behavioral proposition, including those
intentionally left standalone. Candidate overlap is explicit and cannot imply
independent findings when evidence is shared.

Candidate compilation does not accept synthesis or authorize public wording,
publication, persistence, database or production writes, or deployment.
