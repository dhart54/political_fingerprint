# Action Interpretability Candidate Authority V1

Date: 2026-08-26

This decision narrowly clarifies the current editorial workflow for Shared Action Core meaning qualification. It narrows only the LLM-role language in `DECISIONS.md` under “2026-02-28 — Summary Generation (LOCKED for MVP)” and “2026-06-05 — Vote Interpretation and Evidence Readiness Guardrails” that could be read to prohibit model-assisted semantic candidate drafting. Historical decision text remains unchanged; the dated superseding entry is appended to that log.

An LLM may propose, explain, challenge, and revise typed, source-mapped, member-neutral action-interpretability candidates from governed exact-action evidence. That work remains a detached proposal. Deterministic code validates structure, exact identity and source binding, mechanical invariants, reproducibility, and non-authorizing state; deterministic success does not establish semantic truth or acceptance.

Neither an LLM nor deterministic code may silently accept a novel interpretation. Member or party identity, official member action, issue eligibility, domain taxonomy, moral or partisan judgment, motive, recommendation, publication, persistence, production, and deployment authority remain outside this permission. Accepted semantics still require the repository's explicit human review and authority path.

When governed evidence cannot support a concrete explanation of the exact action, the candidate must use `source_enrichment_required` or `insufficient_for_useful_interpretation`. Vague filler is not an acceptable substitute. Amendments remain bound to their exact amendment evidence; whole-package actions cannot be projected onto ungoverned components; House outcome does not establish enactment.

This clarification creates no second compiler or governance framework. It operates only at Shared Action Core meaning qualification within the accepted five-layer model and authorizes no current Education candidate, historical-artifact rewrite, public wording change, publication, or production write.
