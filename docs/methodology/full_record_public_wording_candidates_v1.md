# Full-Record Public-Wording Candidates V1

This detached contract turns accepted internal behavioral and synthesis semantics into concise public-copy proposals without changing semantic meaning or granting downstream authority.

Each wording item binds immutable accepted implementation records and their content digests. Behavioral proposition type fixes the public surface: repeated patterns remain repeated patterns, trajectories remain trajectories, and notable choices remain individual choices. Synthesis wording binds accepted synthesis records and preserves their input relationship roles.

New packages use milestone-neutral Behavioral Semantic IR and synthesis
authority/implementation bindings. The frozen M11K vocabulary remains valid for
historical compatibility. Blocked-action boundaries are represented as a list,
which may be empty; the frozen singular M11K representation remains valid and a
package may never invent a blocked action absent from accepted semantics.

Every internal material limitation is accounted for as either retained public copy or an explicitly recorded compression or omission. This makes limitation removal reviewable without pretending that a validator can judge prose quality.

Direction displays use only `+` Support, `−` Opposition, and `±` Mixed. They are optional when one label would obscure the accepted behavior. Raw Yea/Nay never determines a semantic direction, and internal `mixed` metadata cannot replace the accepted proposition content.

Candidate packages, empty decision templates, and parity manifests are non-authorizing. Human wording review is required before copy can become canonical or production-selectable. Publication, persistence, database writes, production writes, and deployment remain separate false gates.
